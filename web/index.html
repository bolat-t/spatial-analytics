The 2010s burnt 6.9 million hectares, the most of any decade on record, most of it in the 2019–20 season — consistent with hotter, drier fire seasons. Yet by 2009, 92% of the homes beside burnt land today had already been exposed. The fire edge is not new; the homes built along it are.
